About

Yuhang Song 宋宇航

山海亦可平 · Building intelligent agents that can read the world and move through it.

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Liverpool and National Tsing Hua University, working at the intersection of embodied intelligence, deep learning, and autonomous systems.

My research life moves between airvLab at the Digital Innovation Facility and ELSA Lab at NTHU. I founded TOPSOFT Group in 2016 as an international organization for computer science.

I am interested in agents that can perceive, decide, and act with efficiency in the physical world. The long-term aim is to build robust end-to-end navigation systems that feel less like isolated models and more like reliable companions for real environments.

Projects

Research

VEL: Vision-Grounded Energy Critics

Vision-grounded energy critics for physically feasible vision-language-action policies. A compact energy head corrects each action of a frozen VLA with one gradient step, with no rollouts or retraining.

Research

DITA Agent

Depth-Inference Termination Agent for object navigation in AI2-THOR. A judge model trained alongside reinforcement learning decides when the agent has reached its target.

Research

Real-World Aligned Embodied Navigation

An object-goal navigation system that trains a CLIP-guided reinforcement learning agent on HM3D homes in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, with a ROS2 Humble mapping stack shared with the real Husky robot.

Industrial

Supermimic on CRP Humanoid

Taking our in-house humanoid crp from dancing in simulation to walking on a gamepad and playing motions at the press of a button, with world-model policy learning from SuperMimic.

Industrial

RoboJev

Testing the new generation of decision models (Jev, Laya, PlayJev and the OpenAI Decisions API) as the brain of a robot arm for the atomic action of grasping.

Industrial

Dexhand AR Teleoperation

Apple Vision Pro teleoperation of a 7-DoF Rokae AR5 arm and an Inspire dexterous hand, with closed-form redundant IK and servoJ streaming at 8 ms.

Industrial

Prophet System

AI online prediction platform.

Industrial

Husky Road-Marking Robot

LiDAR-guided wall following with PID control for autonomous highway line drawing, developed in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and deployed on a real Husky UGV with ROS1.
